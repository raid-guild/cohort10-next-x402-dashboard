# x402-product-sprint

This directory contains research artifacts for assessing the viability of an x402 facilitator product offering.

Each artifact below represents a research task or deliverable that contributes to the overall product assessment. Once all research is complete, the findings will be compiled into a Raid Improvement Proposal (RIP) for review by official Raid Guild members.

## Artifacts

1. **[ARTIFACT_1](./ARTIFACT_1.md)** - Hackathon Feedback Analysis
   
   Collect and analyze feedback from the upcoming hackathon to inform product improvements and identify cross-promotion opportunities.

2. **[ARTIFACT_2](./ARTIFACT_2.md)** - Market Research: Facilitator Solutions
   
   Conduct market research on existing facilitator solutions, including pricing models, usage metrics, and feature sets.

3. **[ARTIFACT_3](./ARTIFACT_3.md)** - Cost Structure & Rate Limiting Strategy
   
   Evaluate cost structures and determine rate limiting strategies to prevent spam bot abuse and gas drainage.

4. **[ARTIFACT_4](./ARTIFACT_4.md)** - Multi-Network Deployment Analysis
   
   Investigate whether deploying separate facilitator instances per network (e.g., Arbitrum, Optimism) is necessary and sustainable.

5. **[ARTIFACT_5](./ARTIFACT_5.md)** - White-Label Product Feasibility
   
   Research the feasibility of a white-label facilitator product for L2/L3 chains, including branding, maintenance, and pricing models.

6. **[ARTIFACT_6](./ARTIFACT_6.md)** - Vercel Hosting Evaluation
   
   Evaluate the use of Raid Guild's Vercel account for hosting the facilitator dashboard and related documentation.

7. **[ARTIFACT_7](./ARTIFACT_7.md)** - Aztec Noir Integration Assessment
   
   Assess the technical viability of integrating with Aztec's Noir language and shielded transactions within the current EVM-compatible stack.

8. **[PROPOSAL](./PROPOSAL.md)** - Raid Improvement Proposal (RIP)
   
   Create a Raid Improvement Proposal (RIP) outlining the facilitator product vision, hosted on GitHub and linked to the Discord forum for community feedback.
